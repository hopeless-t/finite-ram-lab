# HYP-003 — Initial Fault Order vs Future Semantic Need

> **Status:** FROZEN DESIGN

## Question

Under 160–162 MiB memcg pressure, does residency follow the independently controlled initial fault/touch order even when the application’s future HOT region is assigned independently, and does conflict between those two signals increase future reuse cost?

## Pseudo-Council convergence

The Council converged on a shared-VMA-only design.

### Kernel / VM reviewer

Use one 64 MiB anonymous mapping split into lower and upper 32 MiB halves.

CHAR-002 already showed the fault-order effect survives the shared-VMA layout. Using shared halves removes separate-VMA creation order from HYP-003 and avoids carrying the known creation/address collinearity into the information-gap test.

### Experimental-design reviewer

Independently randomize:

~~~text
MemoryHigh:
160 / 162 MiB

initial fault/touch order:
lower→upper / upper→lower

future HOT position:
lower / upper
~~~

Every runner block executes all eight cells exactly once.

~~~text
2 × 2 × 2 = 8 cells / block
16 runner blocks
128 total trials
~~~

Future HOT identity must not alter any operation before the post-burst residency snapshot.

### Information-theory reviewer

Do not compute a grand Value-of-Information scalar yet.

First establish the same-experiment separation:

~~~text
past OS-visible cue: initial fault/touch order
future app-known cue: future HOT identity
~~~

The two factors are independently assigned.

### Statistics reviewer

Runner is the top-level replication block.

Primary contrast:

~~~text
mean HOT resident fraction when HOT == second-faulted
-
mean HOT resident fraction when HOT == first-faulted
~~~

Positive values mean the future-needed region is more resident when future semantics happen to align with the past fault-order cue.

Primary inference:

- exact one-sided sign-flip over 2^16 block sign assignments;
- 20,000-resample runner-cluster bootstrap interval.

Key secondary contrast:

~~~text
mean log HOT-retouch latency when HOT == first-faulted
-
mean log HOT-retouch latency when HOT == second-faulted
~~~

Positive values mean semantic conflict with the past cue is costly.

Use exact one-sided sign-flip and a cluster-bootstrap geometric-mean latency ratio.

### Manipulation-check reviewer

Also report:

~~~text
resident(second-faulted) - resident(first-faulted)
~~~

before HOT reuse.

This is a manipulation check inherited from CHAR-002, not a new primary discovery target.

### Falsification reviewer

The information-gap interpretation is weakened if:

- the same-experiment fault-order manipulation check disappears;
- aligned and misaligned HOT residency are indistinguishable;
- semantic conflict does not increase reuse cost at the sensitivity of the design.

A positive residency mismatch without a latency effect is still informative but does not establish performance value.

### Monte Carlo reviewer

No design Monte Carlo is used.

Reasons:

- the full 2×2×2 factorial fits in every runner block;
- CHAR-002 already measured a large fault-order residency effect in the exact shared-VMA substrate;
- the experiment is a direct orthogonalization test, not a rare-event search;
- a simulation would require inventing a new latency-noise model when direct balanced evidence is affordable.

## Frozen workload

~~~text
shared mapping = 64 MiB
lower half     = 32 MiB
upper half     = 32 MiB
burst          = 96 MiB
MemoryHigh     = 160 / 162 MiB
MemoryMax      = 320 MiB
~~~

Procedure:

1. allocate one shared mapping;
2. fault/touch both halves in the frozen randomized order;
3. record content-integrity baseline;
4. allocate/touch the 96 MiB burst;
5. observe lower/upper residency before semantic reuse;
6. retouch the independently declared HOT half;
7. record latency and reclaim/refault/swap counters.

## Primary scientific meaning

A supported primary residency contrast would show that application future demand and the kernel-visible historical cue are separable in the same experiment:

~~~text
residency follows past fault/touch order
while
future HOT need is independently assigned
~~~

If the key latency contrast is also supported, that mismatch has direct measured reuse cost.

## No intervention

HYP-003 uses no memory hint or reclaim intervention.

No MADV_PAGEOUT, MADV_COLD, proactive reclaim, mlock, coordinator, or kernel modification.

## Authority boundary

A positive HYP-003 result would establish a bounded information mismatch and its cost in this hosted memcg workload.

It would justify a later formal Value-of-Information analysis, but it would not by itself justify a coordination mechanism or kernel change.
