# OBS-003 — Natural Residency Misalignment Map

> **Status:** FROZEN CONTRACT / NOT YET RUN

## Question

Across the 160–168 MiB transition region, how often does the unmodified NO_HINT workload reach HOT reuse with the future-needed HOT semantic region not fully resident?

## Frozen design

Monte-Carlo-selected design:

```text
32 independent hosted-runner blocks
5 MemoryHigh levels
2 repeats per level per block
320 total trials
```

Levels:

```text
160, 162, 164, 166, 168 MiB
```

Every runner block executes every level in a deterministic shuffled order.

The physical mapping that carries the HOT semantic identity is balanced A/B.

## Workload

Use the established EXP-002/VAL-003 semantic-region workload with:

- region A: 32 MiB;
- region B: 32 MiB;
- one region declared HOT and reused next;
- one region declared COLD;
- burst: 96 MiB;
- no PAGEOUT;
- no MADV_COLD;
- no proactive reclaim;
- no application hint;
- MemoryMax: 320 MiB.

Only MemoryHigh changes across trials.

## Primary observable

Immediately before HOT reuse:

```text
misaligned = HOT resident fraction < 1.0
```

as observed by `mincore(2)`.

For every MemoryHigh level report:

- total trials;
- misaligned trials;
- raw trial proportion;
- mean runner-block misalignment rate;
- cluster-bootstrap 95% interval over runner blocks.

## Secondary diagnostics

Descriptive only:

- HOT resident fraction distribution;
- HOT retouch latency;
- swap present before reuse;
- swap-ins during HOT reuse;
- anonymous refaults;
- major faults.

Also report latency separately for:

```text
HOT fully resident
versus
HOT not fully resident
```

This is explanatory, not a causal intervention comparison.

## Scientific interpretation

OBS-003 estimates how often a **natural residency-selection opportunity** appears under the declared workload.

It does not define the gain of an ideal policy.

A high misalignment prevalence means there is frequent state-selection headroom worth investigating.

A low prevalence means even a perfect residency selector would have limited opportunity frequency under that condition.

## Pseudo-Council boundary

The study must not:

- test a new hint;
- pool CORRECT_PAGEOUT arms;
- call every missing page a kernel error;
- interpret MemoryHigh as literal physical-RAM capacity;
- convert an observed opportunity rate directly into expected production speedup.

## Next step after result

Use the observed prevalence curve and conditional cost distribution to decide whether a formal Value-of-Information / decision-headroom model is warranted.

Do not choose another mechanism first.
